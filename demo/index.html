Strong Physics & Big Dots

<div ko-effect="dots-shader" ko-theme="violet" ko-physics="strong" ko-dot-size="2.5" ko-total-size="20">

Use ko-physics to change fluid intensity, and ko-dot-size / ko-total-size to change density.

Mixed Physics Fine-Tuning

<div ko-effect="dots-shader" ko-physics="subtle" ko-mouse-radius="strong" ko-ripple-speed="strong" ko-ripple-duration="subtle">

Mix preset words per-attribute: wide cursor reach + fast ripple, but gentle force and short ripple life.

No Mouse Interaction

<div ko-effect="dots-shader" ko-theme="mono" ko-mouse="false">

Ideal for ambient background covers. Ignores the mouse and saves on compute power.

Custom Particles
