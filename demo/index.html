KineticOS Attribute Showcase

Hover your mouse over the cards below to see the different attribute setups in action.

Default

<div ko-effect="dots-shader">

Basic dots effect. Tracks your mouse everywhere on the screen (global setup).

Hover Me (Container)

<div ko-effect="dots-shader" ko-hover="container">

The effect stats hidden at 0% opacity. It gracefully fades in only when you hover directly over this card.

Theme: Cyber

<div ko-effect="dots-shader" ko-theme="cyber">

Quickly change the entire color scheme using built-in presets like 'cyber', 'ocean', 'hologram', etc.

Custom Dual-tone

<div ko-effect="dots-shader" ko-colors="#e11d48,#fb923c">

Override any theme with your own hex codes. You can pass from 1 up to 6 comma-separated colors.

Extreme Physics & Big Dots

<div ko-effect="dots-shader" ko-physics="extreme" ko-dot-size="2.5" ko-total-size="30.0">

Use ko-physics to change fluid logic, and ko-dot-size / ko-total-size to change density.

No Mouse Interaction

<div ko-effect="dots-shader" ko-theme="forest" ko-mouse="false">

Ideal for simple background aesthetic covers. Ignores the mouse and saves on compute power.
